2. Description of Service

Pocket Estimate provides AI-powered property damage assessment tools for insurance adjusters, contractors, and property owners, including but not limited to:

  • LiDAR room scanning using Apple's RoomPlan framework
  • Photo capture and annotation with arrows, circles, squares, and highlights
  • Voice dictation and typed scope-of-damage entry
  • AI-assisted damage analysis and estimate generation
  • Low-to-high damage exposure range calculation
  • Itemized repair estimates organized by room and phase
  • Automatic photo documentation sheets
  • Export of reports as PDF or XLS files
  • Report sharing via email and iOS share destinations

AI-generated estimates are reference tools only. They do not constitute a formal appraisal, inspection, engineering assessment, or insurance determination of any kind.
